## What is FactoryPulse AI?


<img width="1079" height="603" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-09 at 21 53 47" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d758588f-2581-411a-85cb-c0282e39872c" />


FactoryPulse AI is an industrial AI platform designed to
predict equipment failures, detect anomalies, and improve
manufacturing reliability through machine learning.

It transforms industrial sensor data into actionable
maintenance intelligence.


## The Problem

<img width="1077" height="727" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-09 at 21 52 09" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/df9071d6-6cea-4a77-8003-be12e92942a5" />

Unexpected equipment failures lead to:
- Unplanned production downtime
- Expensive emergency repairs
- Reduced operational efficiency
- Increased maintenance costs
